bleNIMA, a Bluetooth LE tester for embedded developers.

Scan, connect and test your Bluetooth LE devices with the computer's own Bluetooth, with no dongle to buy: every advert decoded, the GATT table in words, the Zephyr shell over the Nordic UART Service in a real terminal, throughput and latency tests, firmware update over MCUmgr, bleNIMA as a device itself, and Bluetooth mesh: provisioning, configuration and the model clients through a proxy. Free and open source, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

  • macOS may say the app is damaged or cannot be checked: move it to Applications, then run xattr -cr /Applications/bleNIMA.app in Terminal.
  • Linux: make the AppImage runnable with chmod +x.

What it does

  • A scanner that shows it allEvery device the computer hears, its adverts in words and in bytes, the scan response apart, iBeacon, Eddystone and AltBeacon read out, RSSI over time, filters, favourites and CSV.
  • The GATT table in wordsNames for 4000 companies and the standard UUIDs, and values in words: Heart Rate, Blood Pressure, Glucose, Cycling Power, Current Time and many more, the descriptors too.
  • Read, write, notify, plotIn hex, text or any number format, with forms for the standard values such as setting the time or an alert, and a live plot of what comes.
  • The device at a glanceIts name, battery, maker, model, serial and firmware, read by themselves when it connects.
  • A terminalThe Zephyr shell over the Nordic UART Service, or any write and notify pair, in a real terminal, as in terNIMA.
  • Tests with a reportThroughput with lost packets, latency, connection soak tests, and send-and-expect scripts with a pass or fail report.
  • Firmware update over MCUmgrFor Zephyr and nRF Connect SDK: the image slots, test, confirm and erase, and an update that uploads, resets and confirms in one go.
  • bleNIMA as a deviceAn advertiser and a GATT server, from 22 presets or your own services, with live values, to test a phone app or a central's firmware. On Windows.
  • Bluetooth meshProvision devices over PB-GATT with any OOB method, then read their composition, give them keys, bind, publish and subscribe, and talk to their models through a proxy.
  • Pairing and bondsPair and unpair, and the bonded devices, with Windows asking for the passkey itself.
  • The link, as it isThe MTU, the PHY and the connection parameters, with a preset to ask for; active or passive scans, and the Coded PHY where the adapter has it.
