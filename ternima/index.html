NewterNIMA 1.3.0: what changed

terNIMA, a terminal for embedded developers.

Serial, RTT, SWO and TCP in one window, the same on Windows, macOS and Linux. It remembers every tab, the closed ones too, finds your board again when its COM number changes, and reconnects when you plug it back in.

Not signed yet: opening it the first time
  • Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS may say the app is damaged or cannot be checked: move it to Applications, then run xattr -cr /Applications/terNIMA.app in Terminal.
  • Linux: make the AppImage runnable with chmod +x, and to use serial ports, add yourself to the dialout group (uucp on Arch).

What it does

  • Serial, RTT, SWO and TCPEvery serial option, RTT through CMSIS-DAP, J-Link and ST-Link probes with no vendor software, SWO, and TCP or Telnet.
  • Zephyr shell and logsA full VT100 terminal with 24-bit color, and logs you filter by level and module, search and bookmark.
  • Live variablesThe firmware's globals, struct members and array elements by name from its ELF, read as the chip runs, beside the terminal or plotted.
  • A live plotterTime, XY, histogram and FFT charts with a scope trigger and a right axis for values far apart, Arduino Serial Plotter format included.
  • Modbus, and its trafficRTU and ASCII as a master that polls and plots, or a slave that answers, and every frame on the bus, each response under its request.
  • Record and replaySave a session with its timing and play it back later, faster or slower, from any point.
  • Copy, paste and Scroll lockSelect to copy and right-click to paste, as in PuTTY. Scroll lock keeps the screen still while lines keep coming.
  • Macros and sequencesMacros on Alt+1 to Alt+9, and sequences of them sent with a wait, a number of times or until you stop them.
  • RTT and variables while you debugRTT from J-Link's or OpenOCD's own server, and the variables through OpenOCD, while your IDE keeps the probe.
