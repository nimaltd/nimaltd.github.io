netNIMA, a network tester for embedded developers.

Test your networked devices from the computer, with each protocol as a client and as a server: a board can be tried from either side, and a protocol with no board at all. UDP, TCP and Telnet, TLS and DTLS from 1.0 to 1.3 with certificates or pre-shared keys, and on top of them MQTT, CoAP, LwM2M, Modbus TCP, MCUmgr, HTTP, WebSocket, DHCP, mDNS and more. Free and open source, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Not signed yet: opening it the first time
  • Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway. The first tab that listens, such as a server, makes Windows Firewall ask whether other devices may reach netNIMA: allow it on the networks your boards are on.
  • macOS may say the app is damaged or cannot be checked: move it to Applications, then run xattr -cr /Applications/netNIMA.app in Terminal. It asks once to find devices on your local network: allow it.
  • Linux: make the AppImage runnable with chmod +x.

What it does

  • Both ends on one PCEvery protocol has a client and a server in netNIMA, so each one can be tried with no device, and a device can be tested from either side.
  • SocketsUDP with broadcast and multicast, TCP and Telnet.
  • TLS and DTLS of every kindFrom 1.0 to 1.3, with the certificate checked or not, client certificates or pre-shared keys, AES-CCM and the old CBC suites through a built-in OpenSSL, ALPN, a key log for Wireshark, and test certificates made in the app.
  • MQTT and MQTT-SNMQTT 3.1.1 and 5.0, a client and a broker, over TCP, TLS and WebSocket; MQTT-SN, a client and a gateway.
  • CoAP and LwM2MCoAP and CoAPS with Observe and block-wise, over UDP, DTLS, TCP, TLS and WebSocket; LwM2M as a server and as a device, in TLV and SenML, with bootstrap and firmware update.
  • Modbus TCPModbus TCP and RTU over TCP, as a master and as a slave.
  • MCUmgr over UDPFor Zephyr and nRF Connect SDK, with firmware upload.
  • HTTP and WebSocketHTTP and HTTPS, and WebSocket over ws and wss.
  • Speed and round tripsiperf2 and zperf over TCP and UDP, Echo with timed round trips, and Ping.
  • The network's servicesDHCP, DNS over UDP, TCP and TLS, mDNS and DNS-SD, SSDP, NTP, STUN, SNMP v1 and v2c, Syslog, TFTP, FTP and FTPS, and Wake-on-LAN.
  • A form, or typed commandsA protocol tab opens on a form: pick a command, fill in its boxes and send. Text takes the same commands typed, such as pub sensors/temp 21.5. MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, LwM2M, MCUmgr and Modbus have views of their own, each request in a row with its answer under it.
  • The PC's interfacesEvery network card with its addresses, MAC address, gateway, DNS servers and traffic.
